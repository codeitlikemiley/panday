## routing policy

- `default.yaml`: no unreachable rules.
- `dev.yaml`: no unreachable rules.
- `local.yaml`: no unreachable rules.

A finding here is a rule that can never fire, which means traffic is going somewhere other than where the file says. The fix is a YAML edit, and it is left to the human reading this PR — a generator that rewrote routing on its own would be the one component here making a decision nobody reviewed.
